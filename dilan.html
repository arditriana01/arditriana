PERSONAL BRAND

Dilan Abeya Website

Designing a modern personal brand website focused on storytelling, audience engagement, and building a stronger digital presence through high-impact visuals and conversion-focused user experience.

Visit Website

Overview

Dilan Abeya ia a mindset coach, fitness mentor, entrepreneur, personal brand influencer. He created brand with concept: “turning pain into growth”

Besides that, he also developed an AI based digital product called AI Dilan, which is a self-help platform based on conversational AI that can be accessed 24/7. Main target for this website:

  • Young professionals
  • Self-improvement audience
  • Fitness & mindset enthusiasts
  • Global audience
Role

WordPress Developer

Timeline

4 Weeks

Stack

WordPress, Elementor, PHP, LiteSpeed Cache

Technical Challenges

Challenge

Combine between personal branding and AI product SaaS, In 1 website without confusing the user

Solution
  • Split Structure: Brand vs Product
  • Conversion Funnel Optimization
  • UX Simplification

My Solution

To strengthen Dilan Abeya’s digital presence and improve product conversion, I designed a personal branding website combined with a product-driven marketing funnel focused on storytelling, authority, and user engagement.

Brand & Product Split Structure

Organized the website into two main sections: personal branding content including story and philosophy, and product-focused pages explaining AI DILAN, benefits, pricing, and portfolio.

Conversion Funnel Optimization

Built a structured user journey starting from audience pain points, introducing AI DILAN as the solution, followed by benefits, social proof, pricing, and strong CTA placement.

Trust & Authority Enhancement

Increased brand credibility by adding media mentions, certification highlights, and personal authority elements throughout the website experience.

UX Simplification

Improved the overall user experience with cleaner visual hierarchy, simplified layouts, and more consistent call-to-action positioning.

Responsive & Performance Optimization

Ensured the website remains responsive and fast across devices through optimized assets, lightweight sections, and performance-focused implementation.

Key Features

AI Product Explanation Section

Created dedicated sections explaining what AI DILAN is, how it works, its benefits, and showcasing portfolio-related information about Dilan Abeya.

Personal Branding Section

Highlighted Dilan Abeya’s personal story, philosophy, experience, and credibility to build stronger audience trust and emotional connection.

Global Accessibility

Users can access information globally through the website and WhatsApp integration with 24/7 online availability.

Conversion-Focused CTA

Strategic call-to-action placements guide users toward product exploration, contact channels, and inquiry actions.
