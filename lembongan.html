HOSPITALITY WEBSITE

Lembongan Island Beach Villas Website

Designing a hospitality website focused on immersive island storytelling, streamlined booking experience, and stronger emotional connection for travelers seeking a luxury tropical escape.

Visit Website

Overview

Lembongan Island Beach Villas is a beachfront resort villa located in Nusa Lembongan, Bali, offering a peaceful tropical getaway experience surrounded by ocean views and island nature.

The resort focuses on creating a private and relaxing atmosphere through beachfront villas, infinity pool experiences, romantic escapes, and a quieter environment compared to mainland Bali destinations.

Main target audience:

  • International travelers
  • Honeymoon couples
  • Digital nomads
  • Travelers seeking hidden gem experiences
Role

WordPress Developer

Timeline

3-4 Weeks

Stack

WordPress, Elementor, PHP

Technical Challenges

Challenge

Balancing immersive visual storytelling with fast-loading performance for international travelers.

Solution
  • WebP image conversion for lighter assets
  • Lazy loading implementation
  • CDN distribution for global access speed
  • Selective image rendering optimization

My Solution

To improve direct booking opportunities and strengthen the resort’s island luxury positioning, I developed an experience-driven and conversion-focused website centered around emotional storytelling and simplified booking flow.

Strong First Impression

Created a visually immersive hero section using ocean-view imagery and clear messaging focused on privacy, island escape, and tropical relaxation experiences.

Simplified User Journey

Structured the homepage flow into villa overview, island experiences, facilities, testimonials, special offers, and booking CTA to guide users naturally toward reservations.

Emotional Storytelling

Focused the visual presentation on ocean views, sunrise moments, beachfront atmosphere, and romantic island experiences to create stronger emotional engagement.

Booking Funnel Optimization

Added consistent booking CTA across sections, sticky booking buttons, and direct booking engine integration to reduce friction during the reservation process.

Performance Optimization

Optimized performance through image compression, WebP conversion, lazy loading, CDN distribution, and responsive asset handling for global visitors.
