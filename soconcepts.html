SO - Concepts Website

Designing a premium real estate and design studio website focused on brand storytelling, project presentation, and conversion-driven user experience for investors and high-end property clients.

Overview

SŌconcepts is a Bali-based design studio and real estate company specializing in design-oriented property development with a strong focus on architecture, interior design, and landscaping.

The company places design as the core foundation in every process, starting from site selection, architectural planning, interior design, until the final visual experience.

Main target audience:

  • High-end property buyers
  • Real estate investors
  • Design-conscious clients
  • International audience

WordPress Developer

Challenge

Maintaining balance between minimalist luxury design and conversion-focused user experience.

Solution

My Solution

To strengthen SŌconcepts’ premium positioning and improve lead generation opportunities, I developed a brand-first and conversion-driven website focused on storytelling, visual presentation, and investor-oriented user flow.

Strong Brand Narrative

Re-structured the homepage into a storytelling-driven experience including brand philosophy, selected projects, design approach, trust elements, and consultation CTA.

Project as Investment Product

Designed each project page as a visual and emotional sales presentation highlighting design philosophy, concept, uniqueness, and investment value.

Minimalist UX with Clear Direction

Maintained a clean and elegant design aesthetic while improving user guidance through strategic section flow and CTA placement.

High-End Visual Presentation

Implemented full-width imagery, elegant typography, modern grid layouts, and immersive visual storytelling to reinforce the premium brand identity.

Lead Generation Optimization

Added consultation CTA, WhatsApp integration, and inquiry forms to transform the website into a lead generation platform without sacrificing the minimalist design approach.
