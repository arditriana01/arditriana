FITNESS E-COMMERCE

Lebih Cepat Indonesia Website

Designing a hybrid e-commerce and B2B fitness website to improve product discovery, strengthen brand credibility, and generate gym setup consultation leads.

Visit Website

Overview

Lebih Cepat Indonesia is a fitness equipment company founded in 2019 and became an official distributor for global fitness brands such as Again Faster, Concept2, and Assault Fitness.

Besides selling fitness equipment, the company also provides complete gym setup solutions including gym layout planning, equipment installation, and after-sales services.

Main target audience:

  • Gym owners
  • Fitness studios
  • Corporate wellness businesses
  • Individual fitness enthusiasts
Role

WordPress Developer

Timeline

4 Weeks

Stack

WordPress, WooCommerce, Elementor, PHP

Technical Challenges

Challenge

Combining e-commerce product selling and B2B gym setup services in one website without confusing users.

Solution
  • Split navigation between Shop and Gym Solutions
  • Clear CTA placement for different user journeys
  • Structured homepage and conversion flow

My Solution

To improve product sales and strengthen B2B lead generation, I developed a hybrid e-commerce and consultation funnel system designed specifically for fitness equipment and gym setup businesses.

Split User Journey

Organized the website into two main flows: B2C product shopping and B2B gym consultation services to create a clearer user experience.

E-commerce Optimization

Improved WooCommerce product structure with category-based navigation, clearer pricing visibility, better product detail pages, and stronger add-to-cart CTA.

B2B Funnel Development

Built a structured lead generation funnel starting from gym portfolio showcase, services explanation, consultation flow, and quotation request.

Trust Building Section

Added gym project showcases, testimonials, and global brand partnership sections to increase trust and business credibility.

Conversion Optimization

Implemented consultation CTA, WhatsApp integration, sticky contact buttons, and simplified inquiry flow to improve conversion opportunities.
