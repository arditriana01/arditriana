HOSPITALITY WEBSITE

Sadara Resort Website

Designing a modern hospitality website to showcase the resort experience, improve booking accessibility, and create a more immersive user journey for travelers.

Visit Website

Overview

Sadara Resort ia a boutique beachfront resort located in Tanjung Benoa, Nusa Dua, Bali. The resort offers a Laid-back luxury experience with combination of:

  • 81 rooms & suite
  • Beachfront location
  • Spa & wellness
  • Restaurant & beach club
  • Wedding & event services

Their target market:

  • International travelers
  • Honeymoon couple
  • Leisure & luxury travelers
  • Wedding & event clients
Role

WordPress Developer

Timeline

2-3 Weeks

Stack

WordPress, Elementor, PHP, LiteSpeed Cache

Technical Challenges

Challenge

Maintenance balance between "visual luxury (image heavy)" and "performance (speed)"

Solution
  • Images were converted to WebP format
  • Lazy loading was implemented
  • A CDN was used to deliver images faster globally

My Solution

To improve the booking experience and strengthen the resort’s online presence, I redesigned the website with a stronger hospitality-focused user journey, immersive storytelling, and conversion-driven structure.

Homepage Sales Funnel

Re-structured the homepage flow starting from value proposition, resort experience, room previews, facilities, offers, testimonials, and booking CTA.

Experience-Based Design

Focused on showcasing the emotional side of the resort experience including relaxation, beach lifestyle, spa experiences, and romantic atmosphere.

Strong Call-To-Action

Added strategic booking CTA such as “Book Your Stay”, “Check Availability”, and “Get Special Offer” across important sections and sticky navigation.

Offer & Package Optimization

Highlighted promotional packages such as romantic stays, wellness packages, and special offers using urgency-based visual presentation and clear CTA placement.

Performance Optimization

Improved website speed and performance through WebP image conversion, lazy loading implementation, CDN integration, and cache optimization.
